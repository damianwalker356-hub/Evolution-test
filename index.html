Help

Joint: tap empty space to place a joint.

Bone: tap two joints.

Muscle: tap two bone centres.

Move / Select: tap a part, drag a joint to move it, or drag empty space to pan. Pinch-free zoom is in Settings.

Delete: tap a part to remove it, together with anything attached to it.

This build is an editor only. Evolve, Gallery, Skin and Simulations are not implemented.
