Evolution creature workbench

Repository test build: editor only. Creature physics and training are not connected. This is not a completed simulator; no animated or fake learning fallback is provided.

Bone: tap two joints. Muscle: tap two bone centers. Select/move: tap a component or drag a joint. Pan: drag the canvas. Imported decorations are retained but not drawn. The drawing is a design preview, not a physics result.
